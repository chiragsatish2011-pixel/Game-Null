## 1. NARRATIVE FOUNDATIONS & CAMERA ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 Camera System Specification
- **Default Mode:** Freely switchable between **First-Person** (immersive tactile presence) and **Third-Person** (environmental readability, spatial awareness, character performance).
- **Contextual Micro-Investigation:** When interacting with 3D physical props (clocks, lock mechanisms, carbon-slips, mechanical signal levers), the camera smoothly transitions to a focused, tight first-person inspection frame with physical depth of field.
- **Cinematic Transitions:** Major emotional reveals and scene transitions use brief, seamless in-engine camera cuts that anchor on character expression before returning control to the player’s preferred perspective.

### 1.2 Narrative Tone & Pacing Guide
- **Tone:** Grounded, cold, tactile, bureaucratic suspense. Pacing alternates between methodical physical investigation (inspecting wear patterns, cross-referencing maps, navigating damp stone) and sudden, heart-stopping systemic friction (an unexpected knock on a door, an unlisted signal bell clanging in an empty room, a confrontation with a weary inspector).
- **Core Emotional Arc:** Thomas begins with defensive denial, shifts to frantic paternal determination, enters profound historical disorientation, collapses under the crushing weight of his own buried complicity, and ultimately claims moral agency to decide the fate of Vesper.

## 3. ACT I: THE RETURN

### Narrative Purpose
Establish Thomas’s ordinary reality, his mechanical competence, his emotional vulnerability, and the sudden, terrifying collision with bureaucratic erasure. The player experiences the initial friction not as a supernatural monster, but as a chilling administrative fait accompli.

---

### MISSION 01: THE EMPTY ADDRESS (M-01)
- **Starting Location:** Platform 4, Vesper Central Station (03:14 AM, Oct 19, 2004).
- **Prerequisites:** None (Playable Introduction).
- **Player Objective:** Return home to Apartment 4B, 14 Krupp Street, and prepare for Elena's weekly visit.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Switchable 1st/3rd person navigation, tactile inventory inspection (pocket watch, apartment key, railway pass), physical environment interaction.

#### Scene 1.1: Arrival in Vesper
- **Cinematic Intro:** Cold rain splatters against the soot-blackened glass canopy of Vesper Central Station. Heavy diesel locomotive #402 idles on Track 4, venting wet steam into the chilly morning air. The platform is empty except for a lone cleaner pushing a wide broom across wet grey tiles.
- **Player Starts:** Thomas Mercer steps down from the passenger car. He adjusts his woolen scarf, pulls his satchel over his shoulder, and takes out his pocket watch.
- **Interaction:** The watch is ticking smoothly. A button press checks the time: **03:14 AM**.
- **Environmental Clue:** On the platform wall, a newly pasted municipal poster reads: *“STABILITY IS CIVIC DUTY. REPORT ALL UNREGISTERED OCCUPANCY TO THE BUREAU OF MUNICIPAL CONSISTENCY.”*

#### Scene 1.2: The Walk Along Krupp Street
- Thomas exits the station into the damp, fog-choked streets of District 01. The streets are lined with monumental neoclassical limestone tenements. Halogen lamps cast sickly orange circles on the wet asphalt.
- He arrives at **14 Krupp Street**, a four-story residential building built of St. Jude's stone. The brass address plate beside the doorway reads: *“PARCEL 14 — APPROVED RESIDENCES.”*

#### Scene 1.3: The Confrontation at Flat 4B
- Thomas climbs the narrow stone stairs to the fourth floor. He approaches door 4B. The painted black number on the wood is familiar, but the brass doorplate beneath it is missing.
- **Tactile Interaction:** The player selects the apartment key from inventory and inserts it into the brass cylinder lock.
- **The Friction:** The key enters only three-quarters of the way. It binds and refuses to turn. On close inspection, fresh, bright yellow brass filings are visible on the floorboard beneath the lock. The cylinder was replaced hours ago.
- Thomas knocks firmly. Inside, footsteps approach. The lock turns from the interior.
- The door opens on a security chain. A sleepy, middle-aged man in striped cotton pajamas stares out at Thomas with irritation and bewilderment. In the background, a small child cries in a distant bedroom.

```dialogue
HENRIK BAUM
(Squinting through the crack, shivering)
Yes? What is it? It's three in the morning.

THOMAS MERCER
(Voice quiet, polite, but tense)
I apologize for the hour. You're... you're in my flat. My key isn't catching in the lock.

HENRIK BAUM
Your flat? Mister, I don't know who you are, but you've got the wrong floor. This is 4B.

THOMAS MERCER
I know it's 4B. I've lived here for twenty-two years. My name is Thomas Mercer. I'm the signalman at West Junction.

HENRIK BAUM
(Frowning, shaking his head with genuine confusion)
Mercer? Look, friend, I moved in yesterday morning with my family. The Housing Office on Adler Street issued the lease. We've got the stamped papers right on the sideboard.

THOMAS MERCER
(Gripping the doorframe, voice tightening)
Let me speak to the superintendent. Herr Kroll downstairs knows me. He's known my daughter Elena since she was four.

HENRIK BAUM
(Looking at Thomas with a mixture of pity and unease)
Herr Kroll died six years ago, mister. The building manager is Frau Lindt. And there hasn't been a Mercer in this building as long as I've been on the waiting list. Now please, go away or I'm calling the district watch.
```

- The door closes firmly. The deadbolt throws shut.
- **Investigation Gameplay:** Thomas searches the baseboard molding outside 4B. In the shadowed gap where the mopboard pulls away from the wall, he discovers **DOC-003: Cadastral Adjustment Warrant #8492-X**. It confirms that Parcel 14, Flat 4B's tenancy was voided as of 00:01 AM on October 18, and reassigned to Henrik Baum. Thomas's name is completely eradicated from the document with chemical bleach.
- **Mission 01 Completion Condition:** Collect DOC-003 and exit 14 Krupp Street into the alleyway.

### MISSION 02: WEST JUNCTION NIGHT SHIFT (M-02)
- **Starting Location:** West Junction Rail Cut, District 02.
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-01.
- **Player Objective:** Reach the West Junction Signal Tower, confront coworker Goran Lindt, and inspect the official shift logs.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Railway mechanics, mechanical clock repair, audio wire recorder analysis.

#### Scene 2.1: The Foundry Cut
- Thomas walks along the ballast of the freight line into District 02. The pristine neoclassical architecture gives way to towering red-brick locomotive sheds, smoke-stained coal loaders, and iron footbridges.
- He ascends the external timber staircase to the **West Junction Signal Tower (LOC-02-A)**.

#### Scene 2.2: Goran's Panic
- Inside, the tower is warm, smelling of machine oil, tobacco, and hot iron stove pipes. 
- Assistant Switchman **Goran Lindt (`CHAR-LIND-007`)** is sitting at the operator's desk, staring pale-faced at the electric track relay board. He jumps when the door opens, knocking a tin mug of chicory coffee across his papers.

```dialogue
GORAN LINDT
(Gasping, backing away from the desk)
Thomas! God... you shouldn't be here. You shouldn't be anywhere near the line!

THOMAS MERCER
(Taking off his wet scarf, setting his satchel down)
Calm down, Goran. What's happened to Krupp Street? My flat has a stranger in it. The cylinder was recut yesterday.

GORAN LINDT
(Whispering frantically, pointing at the track board)
Don't talk about Krupp Street! Two men from Adler Street were here at midnight. Men in grey tweed coats. They had clipboards, Thomas. They sat right in your chair and went through thirty years of ledger sheets!

THOMAS MERCER
Consistency inspectors? What were they looking for?

GORAN LINDT
Elena! They wanted Elena's address files, her childhood registration slips, anything you kept in your locker. They asked me when you were returning. I told them you were on the night freight from Greyhaven.

THOMAS MERCER
Did they touch my personal locker?

GORAN LINDT
They broke the master padlock, Thomas. They took three boxes of files. But... Thomas, look at the board. Look at Track 3!
```

#### Scene 2.3: The 03:14 AM Phantom Relay
- Goran points to the illuminated track schematic. A red light is blinking on **Track 3 (The Deep Freight Cut)**.
- **Mechanical Puzzle:** Thomas approaches the mechanical lever frame. Track 3 is permanently marked with red lead paint as "DECOMMISSIONED 1976." The interlock lever (#14) is bolted down with an iron collar.
- Yet, outside in the fog, the track relay *CLICKS*. A heavy iron pneumatic air brake exhales in the cut below.
- Thomas looks out the rain-streaked window. In the darkness below, an unmarked freight train rolls by at low speed. The locomotive has no road number; its windows are covered with heavy sheet iron.
- **Investigation:** Thomas rewinds the wire recorder on the desk. He plays back **REC-001**. He hears Goran's panicked voice from minutes earlier, followed by five seconds of static and Elena’s distinctive, quiet humming of an old lullaby.
- **Mission 02 Completion Condition:** Listen to REC-001, confront Goran, and obtain the master key to the signalmen's personal lockers.

### MISSION 03: ELENA'S LOCKER (M-03)
- **Starting Location:** Signal Tower Basements, West Junction.
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-02.
- **Player Objective:** Search Thomas's ransacked locker and uncover what Elena hid before her disappearance.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** 3D prop inspection, seam searching, hidden compartment deduction.

#### Scene 3.1: The Ransacked Locker
- Thomas descends into the ground-floor lamp room beneath the tower. His metal locker has been crowbarred open. Family photo albums have been ripped apart; his letters from Martha are gone.
- But hanging on the coat hook is Elena’s heavy **plum-colored wool coat**. The inspectors ignored it, assuming it was an ordinary worker's spare coat.

#### Scene 3.2: The Hidden Seams
- **Interactive Inspection:** The player examines the plum coat in 3D first-person mode.
- Inspecting the heavy silk interior lining reveals coarse, hand-stitched linen thread along the hem.
- Thomas uses his spring-caliper blade to slit the thread. Inside the lining, he discovers:
  1. **A tarnished bronze key (`KEY-MERC-04`)** stamped with custom clockmaker's notches.
  2. **A railway parcel freight voucher (`DOC-007`)** dated September 26, 2004, addressed to *“Konrad Hess, Bookbindery, Old Canal District.”*
  3. A handwritten slip in Elena's precise, sharp handwriting:  
     *“Father: If you are reading this, Krupp Street is already gone. Do not go to the police. Do not go to Adler Street. Konrad has the duplicate sheets. The lock on Vault 04 is set to your #4 winding tooth. Remember what you told me: 'If a gear sticks, you don't force it; you find the grit.' — E.”*
- **Cinematic Beat:** Footsteps crunch on the ballast outside. Headlights sweep across the frosted glass windows of the lamp room. A beige municipal van has pulled up outside the tower.
- Thomas pockets the key, slips through the rear coal chute, and vanishes into the darkness of the rail cut.
- **Mission 03 Completion Condition:** Escape the rail yards into the streets of District 03 without being spotted by Bureau searchlights.

## 4. ACT II: THE INCONSISTENCY

### Narrative Purpose
Expand the scope of the mystery from Thomas’s personal eviction to the realization that an entire demographic has been systematically erased. Thomas travels between the urban counter-archives of the Old Canal and the rural memory reservoir of Greyhaven, discovering that Elena was not running away—she was building a fortress of proof.

### MISSION 04: THE OLD CANAL PRINTSHOP (M-04)
- **Starting Location:** Lower Canal Quays, District 03.
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-03.
- **Player Objective:** Locate Konrad Hess at his basement workshop and learn the nature of Elena's stolen records.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Stealth, environmental navigation, multi-branch dialogue, document analysis.

#### Scene 4.1: Hess & Sons Bookbindery
- Thomas navigates the damp, narrow alleys of District 03, crossing the stone canal bridges where barges laden with coal idle in the mist.
- He enters **LOC-03-A: Hess & Sons Bookbindery**. The workshop is crammed with cast-iron nipping presses, rolls of bookcloth, and vats of hide glue.
- **Konrad Hess (`CHAR-HESS-005`)** is working late under an electric drop-light, shaving the edges of an oak board with a drawknife. When Thomas steps into the light, Hess recognizes him instantly from family photos Elena kept.

```dialogue
KONRAD HESS
(Setting the drawknife down, wiping his ink-stained hands)
You have her eyes, Thomas. The same grey stone. I told her you'd come. She said you were too stubborn to stay in your tower.

THOMAS MERCER
Where is she, Konrad? The Bureau ransacked Krupp Street. They recut my lock.

KONRAD HESS
(A bitter, rasping chuckle)
They didn't just recut your lock, my friend. They struck your name from the tax register. You don't exist on Adler Street anymore. Just like my wife in '67. Just like thirty thousand others.

THOMAS MERCER
Elena left a note about duplicate sheets. What did she have you bind?

KONRAD HESS
(Leaning forward, his voice dropping to a gravelly whisper)
The truth, Mercer. Written in carbon that doesn't wash out, bound in cold lead that doesn't shake when their damn turbines spin. Two volumes. Fourteen hundred names. She called it The Black Registry.

THOMAS MERCER
Where are the books?

KONRAD HESS
She took Volume II down into the Deep with her three weeks ago, before the cordon closed. But Volume I... Volume I is hidden where their surveyors can't reach. In your family's old split-house across the ridge in Greyhaven.
```

- **Complication:** Heavy fists hammer on the street-level shop door. A voice shouts: *“Bureau of Consistency! Open the premises for cadastral audit!”*
- Hess gestures toward the rear canal cellar. *“Take the coal skiff, Thomas. Go to Greyhaven. Talk to Martha’s sister Lidia. She’s the only one left who remembers what Vesper was before the settlement.”*
- **Mission 04 Completion Condition:** Evade Inspector Pavel Radek's search squad via the canal tunnel and board the morning worker's train to Greyhaven.

### MISSION 05: THE SPLIT HOUSE OF GREYHAVEN (M-05)
- **Starting Location:** Greyhaven Village Outskirts (28 km southwest of Vesper).
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-04.
- **Player Objective:** Reach the Mercer ancestral cottage, meet Aunt Lidia, and locate Volume I of *The Black Registry*.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Rural environmental exploration, spatial puzzle (the Split House), cassette player analysis.

#### Scene 5.1: The Silence of Greyhaven
- Thomas arrives in Greyhaven. The atmosphere is starkly different from Vesper: crisp coastal air, towering wet pines, the scent of burning peat and salt spray.
- Most importantly, the persistent, low-frequency acoustic drone of Vesper is **absent**. Thomas experiences a physical sensation of sudden mental clarity and silence.
- In the village, older fishermen nod to him. They remember his father Arthur; they remember Elena visiting throughout 2003. Here, human memory has not been dulled by the city’s infrasonic ventilation network.

#### Scene 5.2: The Split House (LOC-GH-04)
- Thomas walks up the muddy forest track to the Mercer ancestral cottage.
- **Environmental Anomaly:** The house is physically split in its reality state:
  - *The East Wing:* Dilapidated, empty, with sagging rafters, damp peeling wallpaper, and cobwebs. It represents the space Thomas abandoned after Martha’s death in 1982.
  - *The West Wing:* Warm, dry, and maintained. Inside, candles are burning; an electric kettle sits on an iron hotplate powered by a bank of storage batteries; and maps of Vesper's subterranean drainage are pinned to the walls with brass tacks.

#### Scene 5.3: Aunt Lidia's Confrontation
- **Lidia Vane-Mercer (`CHAR-AUNT-008`)** enters the cottage, carrying an armload of dry pine firewood. She stares at Thomas with unflinching, sorrowful eyes.

```dialogue
LIDIA VANE-MERCER
(Dropping the firewood into the woodbox)
Thirty-six years, Thomas. Thirty-six years you hid in that signal tower, polishing your brass clocks while they paved over the dead.

THOMAS MERCER
(Voice cracked, looking at the floor)
Lidia, I... I had to raise Elena. If I spoke, they would have taken her.

LIDIA VANE-MERCER
And she grew up and did what you were too terrified to do! She came here six months ago with blood on her collar, carrying two heavy lead folios. She said, 'Aunt Lidia, my father thinks he can keep the world running on time, but the tracks are built over mass graves.'

THOMAS MERCER
Where is the first book?

LIDIA VANE-MERCER
(Pointing with her walking stick toward the stone hearth)
Under the hearthstone. Where your father kept the union strike funds in '65. Lift the granite slab, Thomas. Look at what your daughter saved for you.
```

- **Investigation Puzzle:** Thomas uses a cast-iron fire poker to lever up the hearthstone. In the dry cavity below lies **DOC-005: The Black Registry (Volume I: Cadastral Redactions 1968–1990)**.
- **Discovery:** Inside Volume I are detailed municipal blueprints of **District 09** prior to its demolition. Glued inside the front cover is **REC-002: Elena’s Field Memo #03**.
- **Audio Clue (REC-002):** Elena’s recorded voice explains that District 09’s subterranean rail sidings were not completely sealed; they can be accessed via **Platform 0** beneath Vesper Central Station, behind the false brick firewall of Metro Line 2.
- **Mission 05 Completion Condition:** Retrieve Volume I, listen to REC-002, and return to Vesper via the freight line before the Bureau seals the regional borders.

## 5. ACT III: THE RESIDUAL

### Narrative Purpose
Shift the investigation into the subterranean necropolis of The Deep. The player discovers the physical reality of the erased city and encounters the first undeniable clues connecting Thomas's youthful railway service to the 1968 liquidations.

---

### MISSION 06: PLATFORM ZERO (M-06)
- **Starting Location:** Metro Line 2 Tunnel, District 01 Sub-Surface (-15m).
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-05.
- **Player Objective:** Infiltrate the active subway tunnels, trigger the emergency air brakes, and breach the brick firewall into Platform 0.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Stealth timing, mechanical railway sabotage, sledgehammer wall breaching.

#### Scene 6.1: The Midnight Line
- Thomas returns to Vesper under cover of darkness. Armed with his railway pass and master track key, he slips into the maintenance shafts of Metro Line 2 beneath Central Station.
- Using Elena's schematics from Volume I, he locates the exact track section where the tunnel wall transitions from modern precast concrete to vintage 1912 glazed white tiles.

#### Scene 6.2: Breaching the Firewall
- Thomas uses a track worker’s chisel and heavy iron sledgehammer to break through a double-course brick bulkhead disguised as a ventilation shaft.
- He steps through the hole into **Platform 0**.
- **Environmental Storytelling:** Platform 0 is a time capsule from November 1968:
  - Enamel signs read: *“PLATFORM 0 — DISTRICT 09 DIRECT FREIGHT & EMIGRANT TRANSIT.”*
  - A wooden newsstand displays rotting, yellowed newspapers dated **November 18, 1968**. The headlines trumpet: *“TREATY OF SETTLEMENT SIGNED — ALL CITIZENS ORDERED TO RE-REGISTER.”*
  - In the dust on the floor are small children’s shoes, discarded suitcases, and broken porcelain teacups left behind during the panic.
- At the end of the platform, an iron staircase leads down into the vertical darkness of Tier 2.
- **Mission 06 Completion Condition:** Enter the freight elevator shaft at the end of Platform 0 and descend to -45m.

### MISSION 07: THE SUNKEN WARD (M-07)
- **Starting Location:** Tier 2 Freight Cut, descending into Tier 3 (-85m).
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-06.
- **Player Objective:** Navigate the subterranean ruins of District 09 and reach the Core Turbine Hall.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Environmental navigation, flashlight battery management, architectural deduction, archival discovery.

#### Scene 7.1: The Cavern of District 09
- Thomas emerges onto a narrow catwalk suspended high above a colossal limestone quarry cavern.
- **Visual Reveal:** Fifty meters below, illuminated only by distant amber inspection lamps, sits **The Sunken Ward**:
  - The crushed stone ruins and intact ground floors of District 09 resting upon the quarry floor.
  - Above the ruins, massive concrete columns soar upward to support the underbelly of District 01’s reinforced over-slab.
  - Street signs, lampposts, and shopfronts emerge from mounds of crushed limestone like ancient fossil beds.

#### Scene 7.2: Primary School #04 & The Freight Wagons
- Thomas descends an iron spiral ladder to the quarry floor. He walks through the silent cobblestone streets of the dead district.
- He enters the half-collapsed ground floor of **Primary School #04**. Inside the principal’s office, he discovers **DOC-004: District Infill Transfer Blueprint**.
- On the rail tracks outside the school sit twelve rusted, wooden livestock wagons.
- **The Suspicion Dawns:** Thomas inspects the brake housing of Wagon #08. Stamped into the cast iron is the maintenance mark of **West Junction Railyards**, with an inspection chalk mark dated **November 1968**.
- Thomas feels a sudden, terrifying spike of vertigo. He remembers the cold wind of November 20, 1968. He remembers his master switchman Karl Richter handing him a clipboard. He mutters to himself: *“No... no, those trains were carrying grain. Richter told me it was emergency winter flour...”*
- **Mission 07 Completion Condition:** Navigate past the wreckage of District 09 and reach the blast doors of the Core Turbine Hall.

### MISSION 08: THE TURBINE CONFRONTATION (M-08)
- **Starting Location:** Core Turbine Hall, Tier 3 (-85m).
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-07.
- **Player Objective:** Cross the Turbine Hall while enduring the acoustic pressure, and confront Inspector Janos Vance.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Infrasound avoidance/stamina management, high-stakes cinematic dialogue, moral cross-examination.

#### Scene 8.1: The Low Hum
- Thomas enters the colossal chamber housing **Rotary Turbines 1 through 6**.
- The physical effect is overwhelming: the massive bronze impellers, each twelve meters across, spin with a slow, hypnotic roar that shakes the marrow of Thomas's bones. The screen vibrates; the audio design is dominated by an oppressive, suffocating low-frequency drone.
- Thomas must move between acoustic baffling shields to prevent vestibular collapse.

#### Scene 8.2: The Meeting with Janos Vance
- Waiting on the central observation bridge is **Inspector Janos Vance (`CHAR-VANC-003`)**, accompanied by two unarmed Consistency surveyors. Janos is holding a thermos and an unlit pipe. He does not draw a weapon.

```dialogue
JANOS VANCE
(Shouting above the turbine drone, his voice ragged)
Turn back, Thomas! There's nothing down here for you but old bones and a prison cell!

THOMAS MERCER
(Holding up DOC-005, his overcoat flapping in the turbine exhaust)
You pushed an entire district down here, Janos! You took thirty thousand people and buried them under a concrete slab!

JANOS VANCE
(Stepping forward onto the metal grating, leaning heavily on the railing)
And what would you have done, Mercer?! In February '67, I pulled my eighteen-year-old brother out of a burning cellar on Adler Street. His skin was stuck to the upholstery! The miners had machine guns in the steeple; the gendarmerie were hanging union boys from the telegraph poles! We were starving, Thomas! We were eating sewer rats and boiled boot leather!

THOMAS MERCER
So you erased them? You made their children forget they ever had parents?!

JANOS VANCE
We gave them fifty years of peace! Fifty years without a single bullet fired in this city! Your daughter wants to tear that slab off and let all that blood pour back into the streets. You think truth heals, Thomas? Truth kills. It killed in '65, and it will kill tomorrow if she opens that vault.

THOMAS MERCER
Where is she, Janos?

JANOS VANCE
(Sighing, looking down into the dark water of the sump)
She locked herself inside Vault 04 three weeks ago. She has the other half of that book. The air scrubbers are running down, Thomas. If you go in there, Director Vane will drop the flood gates and drown the whole quarry. Turn around. Take the morning train to Greyhaven and live out your years.
```

- **Player Choice:** Thomas refuses the compromise. He uses a railway signal flare to blind the surveyors, darts through the turbine maintenance airlock, and descends the final ladder toward Vault 04.
- **Mission 08 Completion Condition:** Evade the Bureau security perimeter and reach the armored bulkhead of Vault 04.

## 6. ACT IV: THE REVELATION

### Narrative Purpose
The emotional and moral core of PROJECT NULL. Thomas breaches Vault 04, reunites with Elena, and is forced to confront the definitive physical evidence of his own historical culpability. His guilt is not a misunderstanding; it is the central moral truth that drives the final act.

---

### MISSION 09: THE THRESHOLD OF VAULT 04 (M-09)
- **Starting Location:** Vault 04 Outer Airlock, Quarry Bed (-110m).
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-08.
- **Player Objective:** Solve the mechanical combination lock on the vault door using the custom winding key (`KEY-MERC-04`).
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Complex physical lock puzzle, acoustic listening pipe mechanics.

#### Scene 9.1: The Armored Bulkhead
- Thomas stands before **Vault 04**, an emergency engineering bunker built into the solid granite bedrock of the quarry wall. The steel door is thirty centimeters thick, fastened with four radial deadbolts.
- Above the keyhole is an acoustic listening pipe. Thomas presses his ear to the brass horn.
- From inside the vault, he hears the rhythmic, slow clinking of a hand-cranked air filter, followed by ragged, shallow breathing. Elena is alive, but her air supply is nearly exhausted.

#### Scene 9.2: The Clockmaker's Lock
- **Interactive Puzzle:** The lock is not a standard keyhole. It is a multi-tier mechanical clock escapement mechanism designed by Elena.
- Thomas must insert **KEY-MERC-04** and use his signalman's spring-caliper to feel for the specific gear teeth. The combination corresponds to the rhythm of the escapement he taught Elena when she was a child: *three beats forward, one beat counter-clockwise, hold tension, release.*
- The radial deadbolts slide back with a resounding metallic *THUD*. The heavy steel door swings open on oiled hinges.

---

### MISSION 10: THE CRUSHING TRUTH (M-10)
- **Starting Location:** Interior of Vault 04.
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of M-09.
- **Player Objective:** Provide water to Elena, inspect Volume II of *The Black Registry*, and confront the documentary proof of Dispatch Order #14.
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Intimate cinematic narrative, documentary evidence cross-referencing, multi-stage dialogue with branching emotional pressure.

#### Scene 10.1: The Bitter Reunion
- The interior of Vault 04 is small, damp, and freezing, illuminated only by a dying battery lantern.
- **Elena Mercer (`CHAR-MERC-002`)** is huddled on an iron cot beneath an emergency foil blanket. Her lips are cracked; her breathing is shallow and wet with the onset of pulmonary edema. But when she sees Thomas, there is no warm tearful embrace. Her expression hardens into an agonizing mixture of exhaustion, fury, and grief.
- On the metal table beside her sits **DOC-006: The Black Registry (Volume II: The Citizen Rolls 1991–2004)**, its lead-bound cover scratched and oxidized.

```dialogue
THOMAS MERCER
(Rushing forward, unclasping his canteen, hands shaking)
Elena! My god, Elena... drink this. Slowly. The air in here—you're freezing.

ELENA MERCER
(Pushing his canteen aside with weak, trembling fingers, voice hoarse and cold)
Don't touch me, Thomas. Put the water on the table.

THOMAS MERCER
(Stunned, stepping back)
Elena, it's me. It's your father. I searched the whole city. Krupp Street was wiped; they recut the lock—

ELENA MERCER
(A harsh, painful cough wracking her chest)
I know what they did to Krupp Street! I worked in the registry for six years, Father! I know every form, every stamp, every lie they tell! Did you think I was hiding in this hole because I got lost?

THOMAS MERCER
We have to get you out! Janos Vance is at the turbines with inspection squads. Director Vane opened the upper sluices—the quarry is flooding!

ELENA MERCER
(Struggling to sit upright, pointing a shaking finger at Volume II)
I am not moving an inch until you open that book. Page 412. Open it!

THOMAS MERCER
(Pleading)
Elena, please, the water is already at the bottom of the ladder—

ELENA MERCER
(Shouting, her voice cracking with fury and tears)
OPEN IT, THOMAS! LOOK AT WHAT YOU DID!
```

#### Scene 10.2: Dispatch Order #14 (The Confrontation)
- Thomas approaches the table. His hands are trembling so violently he can barely turn the stiff, heavy lead cover.
