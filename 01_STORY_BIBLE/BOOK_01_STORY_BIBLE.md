### 7.2 The Outside: Phenomenological & Geographic Classification
Begins at **Kilometer 14** along the decommissioned northern coal spur cutting through the granite bluffs into an unmapped coastal plain:

#### A. Ordinary Geography & Abandoned Infrastructure
- Cold, wind-swept coastal lowland of deep acidic peat bogs, tidal salt marshes, and stunted pine barrens.

#### B. Perceptual & Navigational Effects
- Basalt bluffs contain heavy magnetite iron deposits, creating localized magnetic compass deflection of up to $45^\circ$.
- Total lack of horizon cues and artificial lighting induces severe spatial disorientation and human walking bias.
- Sheer vertical basalt cliffs reflect low-frequency locomotive chuffs with a $7\text{ to }10\text{-second}$ delay.

#### C. Unexplained Spatial Anomalies (Investigative Mechanics)
Repeatable spatial inconsistencies that cannot be explained by ordinary topography:
1. **The Azimuth Deflection Anomaly:** Travelers walking on open, level peat using chained optical sighting stakes (independent of compasses) return to their initial stake after exactly $6.4\text{ km}$ of dead reckoning across flat ground.
2. **Metric Non-Additivity (Defined Surveying Paradox):**
   - *Observable Rule:* In ordinary flat terrain, physical surveying instruments (calibrated theodolites, standardized invar chains) produce consistent Euclidean geometry where any curved route is strictly longer than the straight-line distance between the same endpoints ($d_{\text{curve}} \ge d_{\text{chord}}$). In The Outside, repeatable measurements between identical, fixed bedrock monuments directly contradict this principle:
     - *Fixed Endpoints:* Bronze survey discs `SM-14` (Kilometer 14.00) and `SM-18` (abandoned turnout) permanently pinned into exposed granite bedrock.
     - *Path 1 (Rail Track Odometry):* Following the physical curved steel rails with a calibrated surveyor's wheel from `SM-14` to `SM-16` measures $3.20\text{ km}$, and from `SM-16` to `SM-18` measures $1.10\text{ km}$ (total physical rail path = **$4.30\text{ km}$**).
     - *Path 2 (Direct Chained Line-of-Sight):* Pacing an unobstructed optical straight line across flat, open peat marsh directly between the exact same endpoints (`SM-14` and `SM-18`) using a standardized $20\text{-meter}$ invar chain under constant spring tension measures **$6.00\text{ km}$**. The measured straight optical distance is paradoxically $1.70\text{ km}$ longer than the curved physical track connecting the identical monuments.
     - *Triangulation Metric Violation:* Three stable, level survey stakes ($A$, $B$, $C$) placed across flat marsh using the identical standardized chaining convention yield legs of $A \to B = 3.20\text{ km}$ and $B \to C = 1.80\text{ km}$, yet the direct straight baseline $A \to C$ measures **$6.40\text{ km}$**. This directly violates the metric space triangle inequality axiom ($d(A,C) \le d(A,B) + d(B,C)$, as $6.40\text{ km} > 5.00\text{ km}$). Forward and backward double-chaining with temperature compensation confirms zero sag or instrument drift; the contradiction is an inherent spatial property of the terrain.
3. **Acoustic Desynchronization (The 18-Minute Recurrence):** Impulsive sounds (gunshots, hammer blows) produce an initial local cliff echo ($7\text{ to }10\text{-second}$ delay), followed by faint, identical acoustic replicas repeating through the fog bank at fixed, predictable **18-minute intervals**, persisting for up to three iterations regardless of wind.
4. **Displaced Matter Counter-Anchors:** Street furniture, lampposts, and boundary stones with erased District 09 foundry stamps physically emerge from peat bogs; where they sit, spatial looping is locally suppressed.
- **Investigative Gameplay:** The player uses physical surveying tools (theodolite, brass chains, audio recorder, journal triangulation) to chart and navigate these anomalies.

## 11. THE THREE CANONICAL ENDINGS (THE FINAL ARBITRATION)

### 11.1 ENDING A: PRESERVATION (The Silent Agreement)
- **Moral Choice:** Thomas chooses Elena's physical life over historical justice. Elena is suffering from acute pulmonary edema; without hospital care, she will drown in her own secretions.
- **The Surrender:** Thomas diverts Locomotive #884 to the Adler Street Bureau siding. He surrenders both volumes of *The Black Registry*.
- **Medical Treatment:** Elena is transferred immediately to St. Jude's Infirmary pulmonary ICU. She is placed on high-flow oxygen, administered intravenous loop diuretics (furosemide) to reduce pulmonary fluid, and warmed. She survives physically, but is permanently detained in a guarded sanitarium ward.
- **The Covenant:** Aldous Vane does not brainwash Thomas. He forces him to sign the *Perpetual Cadastral Affirmation* under penalty of treason, swearing Elena's records were forgeries.
- **Consequence:** The 1,418 records are incinerated. Thomas retains his job and pension at West Junction.
- **Epilogue:** Six months later. Thomas sits alone in his signal tower. Clocks tick in perfect synchronization. He remembers every face and every name. He visits Elena monthly at St. Jude's; she refuses to speak to him, turning her face to the wall. Vesper continues in comfortable, terrifying peace.

### 11.2 ENDING B: RECOGNITION (The Broken Consensus)
- **Moral Choice:** Thomas refuses the lie. He accepts that living with state amnesia has destroyed his soul and that Vesper must face the agonizing truth.
- **The Ascent & Traction:** Locomotive #884 climbs the 1:48 grade of the Old Subterranean Mail Line, sustaining 180 PSI boiler pressure. Total traction demand is $\approx 37.2\text{ kN}$ [Engineering Estimate / Idealized Mechanical Calculation]. Thomas operates continuous pneumatic sanding to prevent fatal wheel-slip on damp rails.
- **The Crash & Deceleration:** At 08:30 AM peak rush hour, carrying $9.58\text{ MJ}$ of kinetic energy at $42\text{ km/h}$, Locomotive #884 rams through the timber/brick bulkhead of Freight Shaft 7, decelerating violently over $40 - 50\text{ meters}$ into the luggage bay of **Platform 2 at Vesper Central Station**.
- **Human Reality & Casualties:** Traumatic and violent. Concrete floor slabs buckle; steam lines rupture; Thomas suffers three broken ribs and severe steam scalds; Elena is concussed. Acknowledged civilian casualties: 1 platform baggage handler (Josef Lind) crushed by falling elevator ironwork; 14 commuters lacerated by glass and flying brick debris.
- **The Evidence Scatter:** The postal wagon splits open, dumping 1,418 duplicate carbon records into the concourse. Commuters read the names of their murdered relatives. Railway workers refuse to clear the line. Red Cross and civil transit medics provide emergency triage to Elena and Thomas on the platform in full public view.
- **Epilogue:** Eight months later. General strikes topple the Municipal Directorate. Chief Registrar Aldous Vane and Thomas Mercer stand trial. Thomas is sentenced to twelve years' imprisonment, serving with clear eyes and dignity. Elena attends the public hearings. Workers break up the District 01 over-slab to exhume District 09. The world remembers.

### 11.3 ENDING C: RELEASE (The Unwritten Boundary)
- **Moral Choice:** Father and daughter reject the closed cycle of Vesper entirely—refusing both the state's cruel amnesia and the city's thirst for bloody retribution.
- **The Route:** Thomas diverts Locomotive #884 onto the northern coal spur through the granite bluffs into The Outside.
- **Emergency Field Rescue & Oxygen Reserve Accounting:** Thomas prioritizes temporary respiratory support and thermal stabilization. Elena is suffering from **acute chemical smoke and particulate exhaust inhalation, secondary bronchitis, and environmental hypothermia** (seriously ill, breathless, feverish, coughing dark particulate sputum, shivering and physically exhausted, but conscious and stabilized with temporary field measures).
  - *Oxygen Reserve Specification (Nominal vs. Usable Accounting):* Two portable steel cylinders recovered from the postal van's mine-rescue cabinet. Each cylinder has a nominal **water capacity of 10.0 litres**, stored under a standard service charging pressure of **150 bar**. While the idealized nominal expansion volume is $10.0\text{ L} \times 150\text{ bar} = 1,500\text{ litres}$ per cylinder ($3,000\text{ L}$ total), mechanical regulators fail to deliver positive flow below a residual pressure of $\approx 10\text{ bar}$, leaving $10.0\text{ L} \times 10\text{ bar} = 100\text{ litres}$ unusable dead-space gas per cylinder. Net usable delivered oxygen per cylinder is therefore:
    $$V_{\text{usable}} = 10.0\text{ L} \times (150 - 10)\text{ bar} = \mathbf{1,400\text{ usable litres}} \quad \text{[Calculated]}$$
    Across both cylinders, the total usable emergency reserve is **$2,800\text{ usable litres}$** [Calculated]. Delivered via a **low-flow nasal oxygen cannula** at a rationed delivery rate of **$1.8\text{ L/min}$** ($108\text{ L/hr}$), the two cylinders provide **$25.9\text{ hours}$** ($\approx 26\text{ hours}$) of **temporary field oxygen support** to maintain baseline oxygenation.
  - *Field Support Protocol:* Thomas clears particulate secretions from her airway, props her torso upright at a $60^\circ$ angle against bunker timbers to alleviate respiratory distress, wraps her in dry wool locomotive blankets, and initiates temporary field oxygen support via nasal cannula from Cylinder 1 at $\approx 11:30\text{ AM}$ on Day 1. He deliberately withholds speculative pharmacotherapy, recognizing that this low-flow field arrangement is only temporary supportive care pending clinical evacuation.
- **Physical Reality of The Outside:** Unmapped coastal buffer zone of acidic peat bogs, brackish salt marshes, magnetite cliff deflection, and whiteout sea fog. Features repeatable spatial anomalies: the $6.4\text{ km}$ Azimuth Deflection loop, non-additive milepost distances (odometry $4.3\text{ km}$ vs direct chord $6.0\text{ km}$), and the 18-minute acoustic recurrence.
- **The Stalled Train & Verified Medical Rescue Timeline:**
  - *Day 1, 11:00 AM:* Locomotive #884 travels twenty kilometers until the unmaintained tracks sink $1.2\text{ meters}$ into waterlogged peat marsh. The driving wheels spin, and the train grinds to a permanent halt. Thomas carries Elena and *The Black Registry* to a derelict stone signal hut, starts a peat hearth, and connects Cylinder 1.
  - *Day 1, 18:30 (Early Recognition of Crisis):* Elena remains feverish ($39.2^\circ\text{C}$) with severe bronchospasm and coughing. Checking Cylinder 1's falling pressure gauge (down to $\approx 90\text{ bar}$ after 7 hours of operation), Thomas calculates that field oxygen will only last through midday tomorrow. He recognizes that without professional clinical evacuation, her respiratory compromise and hypothermia will prove fatal.
  - *Day 2, 00:30 AM (Cylinder Switch):* Cylinder 1 empties to its 10 bar residual cutoff after 13.0 hours ($1,400\text{ L} / 108\text{ L/hr}$). Thomas switches smoothly to **Cylinder 2** ($1,400\text{ usable litres}$ at 150 bar, providing another 13.0 hours of delivery until 13:30 / 01:30 PM). He banks the hearth with slow-burning peat sods, wraps Elena securely, and waits for dawn twilight.
  - *Why Help Cannot Be Summoned Sooner:* Northern telegraph lines were severed in 1968; connecting to the municipal grid would alert consistency enforcers; freezing sea fog ("The Grey Sheet") reduces visibility to under 15 meters, making unmapped nocturnal navigation through waterlogged bogs impossible.
  - *Day 2, 05:00 AM (The Forced March):* Cylinder 2 has run for 4.5 hours (consuming $486\text{ litres}$). The pressure gauge reads $\approx \mathbf{101\text{ bar}}$ ($150 - 48.6\text{ bar}$), leaving $\mathbf{914\text{ usable litres}}$ above the 10 bar residual threshold. At $108\text{ L/hr}$, this remaining oxygen will last for **$8.46\text{ hours}$** (until 13:28 / 01:28 PM). Thomas wedges Elena securely upright and sets out at dawn on an arduous $14\text{ km}$ forced march south along the elevated coastal gravel dyke toward **North Quay** (an isolated community of former 1968 deportees).
  - *Day 2, 08:15 AM (Recruiting Aid):* Pushing his 56-year-old body across wet gravel and sea spray despite fractured ribs from the locomotive collision, Thomas reaches North Quay in 3 hours 15 minutes. He recruits **Dr. Erich Klaus** (former chief surgeon at Vesper Railway Infirmary living under an unlisted identity) and **Sister Johanna**.
  - *Day 2, 08:15 – 09:00 AM (Mobilization):* Klaus and Johanna harness a sturdy two-horse flat marsh wagon loaded with transportable clinical oxygen cylinders, a foot-operated mechanical suction pump, sterile airway equipment, and warm wool blankets (45 minutes).
  - *Day 2, 09:00 AM:* The rescue wagon departs North Quay heading north along the coastal causeway.
  - *Day 2, 11:15 AM (Timely Rescue Arrival & Positive Reserve):* The rescue wagon arrives at the stone signal hut after 2 hours 15 minutes of travel ($14\text{ km}$ at $\approx 6.2\text{ km/h}$).
    - *Oxygen Reserve at Arrival:* During Thomas's 6.25-hour absence (05:00 to 11:15 AM), Cylinder 2 consumed $6.25\text{ h} \times 108\text{ L/hr} = 675\text{ litres}$.
    - *Remaining Reserve:* Cylinder 2 has **$\mathbf{239\text{ usable litres}}$** remaining ($914 - 675\text{ L}$) with a positive gauge pressure of $\approx \mathbf{34\text{ bar}}$ ($10\text{ bar residual} + 23.9\text{ bar}$), providing **$\approx 2\text{ hours } 13\text{ minutes}$** of continuous positive pressure remaining when Dr. Klaus enters the shelter [Calculated]. Elena is never deprived of supplemental oxygen.
  - *Clinical Handover & Medically Supervised Care:* Dr. Klaus and Sister Johanna immediately transition Elena from temporary field oxygen to medically supervised respiratory support and clinical warming, stabilizing her condition for eventual evacuation. Over the subsequent 72 hours, she receives continuous bedside nursing and professional clinical supervision until her spontaneous air exchange stabilizes and her fever breaks.
- **Clinical Outcome & Irreversible Cost:** Elena survives through timely intervention, but her recovery carries real physical trauma: severe particulate chemical smoke causes permanent **bronchial hyperreactivity, chronic exertional breathlessness, and reduced vital capacity**, leaving her with a persistent cough and breathlessness in damp or freezing weather.
- **Epilogue:** Months later, Thomas and Elena sit beside the small hearth. Elena breathes slowly, wrapped in a coarse wool shawl. They have no money, no home, no legal existence, and no citizenship. But for the first time in thirty-six years, Thomas sleeps without nightmares.
