## 1. PREFATORY NOTE ON CANONICAL TRUTH

This document constitutes the definitive historical truth of the PROJECT NULL universe. It records what actually occurred in the physical world, independent of state propaganda, redacted public archives, or flawed human memory.

The events described herein are divided into five epistemic layers:
1. **The Objective Truth:** What physically and historically transpired.
2. **The Public Consensus (The Known):** What the citizens of Vesper believe to be true.
3. **Institutional Knowledge (The Bureau):** What the administrative directors know and enforce.
4. **Thomas Mercer’s Memory:** What the protagonist remembers at the opening of the story.
5. **The Player’s Trajectory:** The sequence in which these layers are unraveled during gameplay.

### ERA 1: THE FOUNDATION & GEOLOGICAL EXPLOITATION (1885 – 1945)

#### 1885–1910: The Discovery of the Ironstone
The city of Vesper was founded at the confluence of the Vesper and Blackwood rivers, situated within a deep geological depression known as the Vesper Basin. In 1885, civil engineers excavating rail cuts discovered a dense, dark-grey sedimentary rock interlaced with oxidized iron veins and micro-crystalline calcite, designated **St. Jude’s Ironstone**.

St. Jude’s stone possessed unprecedented structural density and compressive strength, but stonemasons quickly noted unusual physical characteristics:
- The stone was highly piezoelectric, generating subtle electric potentials when subjected to mechanical vibration and acoustic pressure.
- Structures built with St. Jude’s stone displayed anomalous thermal and acoustic retention: buildings remained warm long after fires died and carried distinct structural reverberations that mimicked the footsteps and voices of their inhabitants.
- When quarried in large volumes, the underlying strata left vast, dry limestone caverns beneath the city—a subterranean void that would later be named **The Deep**.

#### 1911–1945: The Metropolitan Expansion
By 1920, Vesper was a regional titan of heavy industry, locomotive engineering, and textile manufacturing. The entire city center—its residential tenements, neoclassical administrative halls, soaring railway viaducts, and drainage systems—was constructed exclusively out of St. Jude’s stone. 

Because the valley was surrounded by steep granite bluffs, the city grew vertically and downward. Beneath the cobblestone streets, engineers built a three-tier subterranean infrastructure:
- **Tier 1 (Shallow Sub-Surface, -10m to -25m):** Public rail transit, municipal water mains, and pneumatic document-distribution tubes connecting government ministries.
- **Tier 2 (Industrial Rail, -30m to -60m):** Heavy freight rail lines delivering raw coal and iron directly from the deep mines to industrial river docks.
- **Tier 3 (The Deep / The Void, -70m to -150m):** Vast, exhausted limestone quarries, drainage sumps, and abandoned mine shafts.

### ERA 2: THE REGIONAL COLLAPSE & THE WINTER OF ASHES (1946 – 1968)

#### 1946–1964: The Fracturing of the State
Following the Second World War and the subsequent collapse of the central imperial government, the Vesper Basin was left politically isolated. Control of the region was contested by three irreconcilable factions:
1. **The Industrial Mining Combine & Railway Guild:** Controlled the energy, coal, and transport lines. Dominated by conservative industrial barons who ran private armed security brigades.
2. **The Reformist Workers' Assemblies:** Centered in the dense working-class tenements of **District 09** and the riverfront factories. They demanded the nationalization of the mines, agrarian land reform in Greyhaven, and trials for wartime labor exploitation.
3. **The Municipal Directorate & Gendarmerie:** The civic bureaucracy in the city center, which sought to maintain order through martial curfews and emergency rationing.

#### 1965–1967: The Winter of Ashes
In October 1965, a general strike by the Railway Guild paralyzed coal distribution on the eve of an unusually severe winter. Gendarmerie forces fired into a crowd of protesters outside Vesper Central Station, killing twenty-two workers. The city exploded into open urban warfare.

For three years, Vesper descended into a brutal, localized civil war known as **The Winter of Ashes**:
- District 09 became an autonomous armed commune. The municipal authorities severed its water, gas, and coal supplies.
- In February 1967, during the coldest week of the war, Combine irregulars and Gendarmerie units surrounded District 09. Heavy artillery shelled the tenements, causing massive fires. The stone buildings collapsed inward, blocking the narrow alleyways.
- Over four thousand civilians and militia members perished from fire, hypothermia, and starvation. Surviving residents were interned or driven into the remote forests of Greyhaven.
- Mutual atrocities were committed across all sides: summary executions in railyards, factory bombings, and retaliatory assassinations of municipal clerks and surveyors.

### ERA 3: THE GREAT SETTLEMENT OF 1968 (THE GRAND COMPROMISE)

#### November 14, 1968: The Greyhaven Compromise
By late 1968, Vesper was economically and socially bankrupt. The coal mines were flooding, rail tracks were twisted scrap, and disease threatened the entire population. The leadership of the three factions realized that a conventional legal settlement was impossible:
- If tribunals were held, virtually every surviving civic official, guild foreman, and union leader would face the gallows.
- If property restitution were granted, ownership disputes over burned tenements, seized farmland in Greyhaven, and dismantled factories would trigger fresh warfare for generations.
- Society could not heal because the weight of documented guilt was too immense to bear.

In a closed conference at the abandoned Greyhaven Sanitarium, the surviving leaders signed **The Treaty of Civic Reconciliation and Settlement (The Great Settlement)**.

The Treaty rested on a radical, terrifying premise: **The erasure of historical continuity as a civic duty.**

#### The Creation of the Bureau of Municipal Consistency
To enforce the Settlement, the signatories established an independent civic authority: **The Bureau of Municipal Consistency (The Consistency Office)**.
- **The Mandate:** To ensure that the public record, physical architecture, and social consensus remained in absolute alignment. Any person, property, or document that provoked historical friction or reminded the public of the 1965–1968 trauma was designated an **"Inconsistent Element"** and slated for quiet administrative removal.
- **The Philosophy:** The Bureau’s first Chief Commissioner, **Dr. Emil Torvald**, formulated its creed: *"Civilization does not rest on justice, but on harmony. When the past threatens the present, the past must yield."*

### ERA 4: THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE CORRECTION APPARATUS (1969 – 1988)

#### 1970–1975: The Discovery of Inscriptional Plasticity
During the clearing of District 09, municipal architects noted a startling physical phenomenon:
- Sections of District 09 that had been completely stricken from the municipal maps, disconnected from utilities, and ignored by citizens began to exhibit **accelerated structural decay**.
- Because St. Jude’s stone was physically attuned to human habit, vibration, and thermal presence, the total absence of human interaction caused the stone’s cementitious matrix to loosen. Within five years, tenements that should have stood for centuries began settling into the underlying limestone voids of The Deep.
- Conversely, when the Bureau constructed new, sanitized municipal housing blocks and registered them with identical parcel numbers, the population accepted the new addresses with total psychological compliance. The collective desire to forget the war acted as an emotional catalyst.

#### 1972: The Subterranean Turbine Network
To stabilize the city’s water table and prevent the flooded lower mines from collapsing the center of Vesper, the Bureau commissioned the installation of six massive **Rotary Industrial Turbines** in Tier 3 of The Deep.

Designed by mechanical engineer **Arthur Mercer** (Thomas Mercer’s father) and acoustic physicist **Dr. Helena Vance**, the turbines served two functions:
1. **Hydraulic Drainage:** Pumping millions of gallons of acidic coal slurry out of the lower limestone foundations into deep underground subterranean aquifers.
2. **Acoustic-Vibrational Grounding:** The colossal slow-turning bronze impellers emitted a constant, pervasive low-frequency mechanical vibration through the bed of St. Jude’s stone. While not an overt "mind-control beam," this constant vibrational hum had an established physiological effect: it suppressed vestibular disorientation, lowered baseline anxiety, and encouraged cognitive habituation. It reinforced civic routine and made discrepancies easy for the human mind to dismiss.

#### 1976: The Physical Remediation of District 09 & Over-Slab Construction
In the autumn of 1976, the Bureau and the Municipal Works Directorate executed **Operation Clean Slate**:
- Following the 1967 firestorm that destroyed all timber framing and interior floors, municipal salvage crews stripped 42,000 tonnes of iron beams, copper piping, and slate tiles between 1969 and 1972.
- The structurally unstable upper masonry facades (floors 3 and 4) were dismantled and pushed into the adjacent **Quarry Pit 03 (St. Jude Open Cut)**. This involved exactly **319,200 tonnes** (recorded in municipal ledgers as ~320,000 tonnes) of broken limestone rubble, representing approximately **210,000 m³** of bulk material.
- Because Quarry Pit 03 possessed a total excavated void volume of **4,200,000 m³**, the 210,000 m³ of rubble occupied only ~5% of the quarry's capacity, forming a compacted talus bed along the lower eastern benches (-70m to -58m).
- The ground-floor stone facades, basement vaults, and street paving remained largely intact on the limestone floor. Above these ruins, engineers sank 120 reinforced concrete support pillars (2.5m diameter) down to solid granite bedrock, bridging the void with a 1.5m to 3.0m pre-stressed concrete over-slab.
- Atop this slab, two meters of imported loam was laid down to construct **Vesper District 01 (The Park District)**—an immaculate civic sector of ministries and boulevards.
- Beneath the slab, the cavernous, un-filled void (over 3.9 million m³) remained dark, dry, and hollow: a subterranean ruin zone designated in engineering schematics as the Lower Void, and known colloquially as **The Sunken Ward**.

### ERA 5: THE EMERGENCE OF THE RESIDUAL & THE BLACK REGISTRY (1989 – 2004)

#### 1989–2001: The Accumulation of Subterranean Pressure
By 1990, the Bureau of Municipal Consistency was no longer merely maintaining the 1968 Settlement; it had become an autonomous, self-perpetuating bureaucratic machine. Whenever an individual protested an unrecorded tax debt, contested a property boundary, or exhibited signs of historical trauma, the Bureau issued a **Cadastral Rectification Notice**:
- The citizen’s birth record was redacted from the Central Registry.
- Their apartment lease was cancelled and reassigned.
- Mail delivery stopped.
- Consistency inspectors politely visited their workplace, presented a severance voucher, and escorted them to the city boundary.

However, the universe was subject to **The Conservation of Displaced Mass**:
- Erased buildings and excised citizens did not vanish from the material universe. Their belongings, their physical bodies, and their traces were physically displaced into the dark recesses of The Deep or driven into the unmapped borderlands of **The Outside**.
- By 2000, Tier 3 of The Deep was crammed with discarded lives: miles of dark, intact streets, shuttered shops, and abandoned rail carriages sitting in perpetual silence beneath the feet of modern citizens.
- Localized cadastral tremors began shaking Vesper. Mortar cracked in clean apartments; shadows in doorways occasionally detached from the frames; and unlisted train relays triggered at odd hours of the night.

#### 2002–2004: The Rebellion of Elena Mercer
In 1998, twenty-year-old **Elena Mercer** was appointed Senior Clerk in the Central Hall of Records. Meticulous, brilliant, and possessed of a ferocious sense of justice inherited from her mother, Elena was assigned to the "Null File" division—the office responsible for cross-referencing and incinerating records of citizens slated for cadastral rectification.

In March 2002, Elena noticed a recurring mathematical anomaly in the ledger books:
- The municipal census claimed Vesper’s population was exactly 420,000—a number that had remained suspiciously static for twelve consecutive years despite fluctuating birth and death rates.
- Cross-referencing utility meters against property maps, she discovered that over **14,000 rooms and apartments** across Vesper were officially listed as "Structural Air Voids" or "Ventilation Shafts"—yet their electric meters showed steady, active electrical draw.
- People were living inside spaces the city insisted did not exist.

Elena realized that the Bureau was steadily shrinking the boundaries of recognized reality to maintain the illusion of complete stability. Rather than burning the de-registration files, Elena began a covert two-year operation to document the erasure.

### 3.1 Thomas Arthur Mercer: Complete Profile

#### Identity & Physical Description
- **Current Age:** 56 (in October 2004).
- **Physical Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, weathered, with deep-set grey eyes and hair graying at the temples. His hands are calloused, permanently stained with machine oil and brass polish, with several small burn scars from electrical relays. He walks with a slight, rolling gait common to railway track-walkers.
- **Attire:** A heavy charcoal-wool railway inspector’s overcoat with dull brass buttons, a dark knitted wool scarf, sturdy steel-toed work boots, and an oiled canvas satchel containing his pocket ledger, a brass spring-caliper, and a mechanical winding key.

#### Psychological Profile
- **Core Traits:** Methodical, stoic, acutely observant, empathetic but emotionally uncommunicative.
- **The Engine of His Soul:** Routine and maintenance. Thomas believes that the world is inherently fragile and that only constant, quiet care prevents things from breaking. He feels more comfortable speaking through the repair of a jammed brass clock than through direct emotional conversation.
- **Guilt & Complicity:** Thomas harbors a deeply buried, toxic trauma: during the 1968 Settlement, as a terrified 20-year-old apprentice switchman, he personally threw the iron ground-switches at West Junction that diverted the troop and freight trains carrying District 09 deportees into the subterranean sidings of The Deep. To survive the guilt, Thomas spent thirty-six years burying himself in the mechanical minutiae of train schedules, telling himself that as long as the trains ran on time, he was innocent.
- **Cognitive Vulnerability:** Thomas suffers from selective memory blunting. Decades of living near the railway transformers and breathing the coal dust and stone air of the lower tracks have made his recall of his own youth hazy and fragmented. He compensates by keeping meticulous, handwritten physical notebooks documenting every train number, track temperature, and switch alignment.

#### Identity & Physical Description
- **Current Age:** 26 (in October 2004).
- **Physical Appearance:** Lean, sharp-featured, with dark brown hair cut practical and chin-length. She inherited her father’s intense, observant grey eyes, but her expression is characterized by fierce intellectual curiosity and quiet defiance.

She realized that the entire peace of Vesper was built on a cowardly bargain: the older generation had traded truth for comfort, and her generation was being slowly devoured by the resulting lies.
- **Her Agency:** She did not run away, nor did she wait for someone to save her. She acted as an **underground archivist**:
  1. She systematically stole and carbon-copied 1,418 de-registration orders.

  4. When the Bureau finally moved to seize her on September 28, 2004, Elena made a deliberate, strategic sacrifice: she lured the Consistency inspectors away from her hidden caches and barricaded herself inside the sealed Vault 04 in The Deep, setting a mechanical lock that only her father’s unique signalman’s winding key could open.

#### What Physically Happens When an Address is Erased
1. **Administrative Voiding:** The Bureau issues a Cadastral Warrant, striking the property from tax rolls, postal routes, and utility connections.
2. **Physical Eviction & Isolation:** Doors are deadbolted, utilities severed, and hoardings erected.
3. **Physical Decay:** Deprived of human heating and structural maintenance, St. Jude's stone weathers rapidly in the basin's acidic dampness, settling into the porous karstic limestone below.
4. **Limits & Failure Conditions:**
   - The phenomenon **cannot** instantly vaporize matter or alter atomic chemistry.
   - It **does not affect every building equally**: structures founded on deep granite, structures containing non-reactive metals (lead, bronze), and buildings with continuous human presence remain completely stable.
   - The process is reversible by re-inhabiting the space and re-establishing physical and institutional records.

---

### 4.2 Failure Conditions, Physical Limitations & The "Leakage"

The mechanism is not omnipotent; it has hard physical limits:

1. **The Law of Material Inertia:**  
   Materials that contain no St. Jude’s ironstone—specifically **cast bronze, virgin rolled lead, vulcanized rubber, and silver-halide photographic emulsions**—are entirely immune to inscriptional decay. A bronze plaque will retain its engraved words even if buried in mud for a century. A lead-sheathed book cannot be faded.
2. **The Spatial Saturation Limit:**  
   The Deep is a finite physical volume. Over forty years of dumping demolished buildings and de-registered property has filled Tier 3 to capacity. The displaced mass is pushing upward against the street foundations.
3. **The Cadastral Tremor (The Leakage):**  
   When a strong physical anchor (such as *The Black Registry*) is brought into an erased space in the presence of someone who remembers the truth, a **Cadastral Tremor** occurs. The local consensus fractures:
   - Mortar turns to dry powder and sifts from the ceiling.
   - Electric lights flicker violently as wiring grounded in the ironstone loses stability.
   - Acoustic echoes decouple from immediate sounds (a footstep echoes two seconds after the player stops moving).
   - In extreme cases, the boundary into **The Outside** tears open, revealing non-Euclidean spatial folds where rooms loop into themselves.

### 5.1 Physical Nature & Structure
The Black Registry consists of two oversized folio volumes. Because it is bound in pure sheet lead, it is impervious to the vibrational dampening of The Deep and blocks chemical fading of inks.

- **Volume I: Cadastral Redactions (1968–1990):**  
  Contains 412 detailed architectural and railway surveys of **District 09**, including the exact blueprints of the subterranean sidings, the sealed passenger platforms, and the drainage sluices beneath Vesper Central.
- **Volume II: The Citizen Rolls (1991–2004):**  
  Contains 1,418 carbon-transfer duplicate receipts of erased birth certificates, marriage licenses, and burial permits stolen directly from the Hall of Records. Each slip contains:
  - Full legal name and original citizen identification number.
  - Last known registered address.
  - Date and hour of Bureau de-registration.
  - The signature of the authorizing Consistency Inspector.
  - In many cases, a small silver-gelatin passport photograph stapled to the upper corner.

### 5.2 Strengths & Absolute Limitations in Gameplay
The Black Registry is a vital investigative instrument, **not a magical talisman**:

- **What It Can Do:**
  - *Reveal Physical Truth:* By cross-referencing an unlisted address in the Registry against Thomas’s current municipal map, the player reveals hidden doorways, sealed stairwells, and bricked-up windows.
  - *Break Bureaucratic Denial:* Presenting a specific stamped carbon receipt to an NPC or low-level Bureau clerk shatters their conditioned denial, forcing them to acknowledge the person or property.
  - *Unlock Subterranean Routing:* The mechanical track schematics in Volume I allow Thomas to calculate switch timings and restore power to the forgotten rail spurs leading into The Deep.
- **What It Cannot Do:**
  - *It Cannot "Bring People Back":* Finding an erased person's name in the Registry does not resurrect them; if they were deported or died in the subterranean shafts, they remain dead.
  - *It Cannot Stop Physical Demolition:* If a Bureau crew is actively sledgehammering a wall, waving the book at them will not stop the bricks from falling.
  - *It Is Heavy & Fragile:* The book cannot be casually carried everywhere. In gameplay, it is stored in Thomas's secure locker at the West Junction Signal Tower; Thomas extracts specific folios, carbon slips, and maps to take into the field.
