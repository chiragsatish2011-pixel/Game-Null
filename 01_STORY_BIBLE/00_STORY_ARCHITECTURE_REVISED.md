## 1. SEPARATION OF APPROVED CANON VS. PROPOSED CANON

To maintain absolute documentary integrity, the universe is strictly partitioned into two categories:

### 1.1 Approved Canon (Non-Negotiable Foundations)
These items are established by the Master Project Charter and are permanently locked:
1. **The Core Axiom:** *"The world doesn't remember you."*
2. **The Phenomenon Paradigm:** The world is not ending; it is being corrected, rewritten, or reconciled. It functions normally on the surface while contradictions quietly exist.
3. **The Four Geographic Environments:** 
   - *Vesper City:* Metropolitan consensus, functioning bureaucracy, residential/commercial normalcy.
   - *Greyhaven:* Rural settlement, stubborn generational memory in soil, stone, and family kin.
   - *The Deep:* Subterranean industrial, research, and transit infrastructure.
   - *The Outside:* Non-Euclidean spatial collapse where unreconciled geometry and displaced reality leak.
4. **The Three Ontological Layers:** 
   - *The Known:* Accepted reality (records, institutions, collective history).
   - *The Residual:* Physical, informational, and emotional traces left behind by conflicting versions.
   - *The Unwritten:* Events, identities, and possibilities not represented in accepted history.
5. **Protagonist Archetype:** "The Returning Man" (ordinary human, observant, analytical, empathetic, memory gaps, distrustful). Progression: Certainty → Suspicion → Disorientation → Recognition → Responsibility.
6. **The Missing Person Archetype:** "The Absence" (central emotional core, real personality, personal history, agency, flaws, independent motivations).
7. **Narrative Structure:** Five Acts (The Return, The Inconsistency, The Residual, The Revelation, The Choice).
8. **Endings Direction:** Preservation, Recognition, Release.
9. **Core Engine & Camera:** Godot Engine on Windows PC; switchable First-Person and Third-Person perspective.
10. **Production Mandates:** Premium Vertical Slice first (Vesper — District 01); strictly Blender-free asset pipeline.

### 2.1 Decision 1: The Protagonist (The Returning Man)

#### Current Proposal: Daniel Vance (Civil Surveyor / Archival Auditor)
- **Why it exists:** Provides an organic explanation for why the protagonist knows municipal layouts, can read architectural blueprints, and has legitimate access to city archives.
- **Narrative purpose:** The investigator uncovers that his own historical survey work was used to demarcate areas that were later erased.
- **Weaknesses:** Leans toward the familiar "amnesiac detective / guilty investigator" trope (e.g., *Disco Elysium*, *Angel Heart*). An intellectual surveyor can feel emotionally detached, turning early gameplay into dry paperwork inspection rather than visceral human panic.

#### Alternative A: Thomas Mercer — Municipal Railway Signalman & Clock Technician (RECOMMENDED)
- **Creative Direction:** Working-class, tactile, physical routine. Thomas is not an elite official; he operates the mechanical switching tower at Vesper West Junction and maintains the mechanical station clockwork.
- **Narrative Hook:** His job is tracking physical arrivals against mechanical dispatch registers. He is grounded in iron levers, telegraph tickers, and timestamps. He notices a four-minute gap in the daily 03:14 AM rail timetable where track relays trigger, signal lanterns switch to red, and air brakes hiss in the empty tunnel—yet the official ledger says no train ran.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intensely human. He returns to his modest apartment after an overnight shift to find someone else’s coat in his closet, his key sticking in the brass lock, and his daughter’s/sister's room converted into a walk-in larder that looks twenty years old.
- **Gameplay Potential:** High tactile interactivity. He understands relays, circuits, switches, mechanical locks, track diagrams, and pocket watches.

#### Alternative B: Julian Karr — Secondary School Literature & History Teacher
- **Creative Direction:** Intellectual, cultural, and deeply tragic. Julian teaches regional folklore and literature at an ordinary civic academy.
- **Narrative Hook:** Julian notices cultural amnesia in real time: a poem by a local poet has its final stanza missing in new printings; a bronze monument in the square has a plaque dedicated to a battle that none of his textbooks mention; an empty desk in his homeroom classroom sits with carved initials, yet his grade register shows no student ever sat there.
- **Emotional Resonance:** The existential horror of watching culture and human legacy evaporate while everyone around him smiles and shrugs.
- **Gameplay Potential:** Heavy reliance on reading documents, cross-referencing books, and conversational deduction. Lower physical/mechanical interactivity; risks leaning into "walking simulator" passivity.

#### Comparison & Recommendation
| Criteria | Daniel Vance (Surveyor) | Thomas Mercer (Signalman) | Julian Karr (Teacher) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Originality** | Moderate (Detective trope) | **High (Tactile rail/clock mechanic)** | Moderate-High (Academic) |
| **Emotional Impact** | Cerebral / Guilt-driven | **Visceral, intimate, immediate** | Melancholic / Philosophical |
| **Internal Consistency** | High | **High (Direct link to The Deep & Transit)** | Moderate (Limited access) |
| **Gameplay Mechanics** | Map reading / blueprint puzzles | **Physical levers, relays, clocks, locks**| Document reading / dialogue |

**Recommendation:** Adopt **Alternative A: Thomas Mercer**. A railway signalman offers the perfect synthesis of ordinary humanity, tactile mechanics (lever towers, telegraphs, signal clocks), and natural access to the subterranean rail lines that lead directly into The Deep.

### 2.2 Decision 2: The Missing Person (The Absence)

#### Current Proposal: Clara Reed (Acoustic Materials Engineer)
- **Why it exists:** Explains how the phenomenon was studied and how counter-measures were engineered.
- **Narrative purpose:** A brilliant scientist who discovered the fatal flaw in the system and left an informational "virus."
- **Weaknesses:** Feels overly sci-fi and tropey. Making her a specialized engineer risks turning her into an untouchable plot device—an exposition dispenser rather than a flesh-and-blood human being whom the player grieves.

#### Alternative A: Elena Mercer — Census Registrar & Civil Records Clerk (RECOMMENDED)
- **Relationship:** Thomas Mercer’s 24-year-old daughter (or younger sister).
- **Creative Direction:** Elena worked in the Vesper Central Hall of Records, stamping birth, death, and residency certificates. She was quiet, observant, and deeply compassionate.
- **Narrative Hook:** Her rebellion was not a high-tech cyber-hack; it was **quiet bureaucratic defiance**. When ordered to pull files for "de-registration," she could not bring herself to incinerate them. She began smuggling carbon-copy registry slips home, sewing them into the linings of winter coats, hiding index cards beneath floorboards, and binding duplicate records into a covert leather ledger (*The Black Registry*).
- **The Tragedy:** She was not kidnapped by monsters. She was quietly called into an administrative office on a Tuesday afternoon, given a severance voucher, and escorted to the door. By Wednesday morning, her neighbors had forgotten her face, her room was remodeled, and Thomas found himself holding a photograph where her face was slowly dissolving into chemical silver gray.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Overwhelming. She represents every individual who refuses to let human beings be treated like disposable ledger entries.

#### Alternative B: Mara Lin — Silver-Halide Photographic Restorer & Printmaker
- **Relationship:** Protagonist’s estranged partner or closest confidante.
- **Creative Direction:** Artist and darkroom technician. She restored old family photographs and civic glass negatives in a basement atelier near the old canal.
- **Narrative Hook:** Mara noticed that when developing certain historical glass plates using silver nitrate, secondary exposure layers ("ghost shadows") appeared—figures standing in crowds who had been airbrushed or chemically bleached from municipal archives. She began creating secret physical prints that preserved these ghosts.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Beautifully visual, poetic, and melancholic.
- **Gameplay Potential:** Strong visual investigation (magnifying glasses, chemical baths, light tables, UV exposure). Slightly less directly tied to the municipal and railway transit systems than a civil records clerk.

#### Comparison & Recommendation
| Criteria | Clara Reed (Engineer) | Elena Mercer (Registrar) | Mara Lin (Photo Restorer) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Originality** | Low-Moderate (Sci-fi tech lead)| **High (Quiet bureaucratic courage)** | High (Darkroom aesthetics) |
| **Emotional Impact** | Moderate (Intellectual peer) | **Devastating (Family bond & defiance)**| High (Artistic romance/loss) |
| **Narrative Integration** | High (Explains the apparatus) | **Flawless (Links citizen to state ledger)**| High (Visual evidence) |
| **Gameplay Synergies** | Tech devices / frequencies | **Hidden registries, carbon slips, keys** | Photo-development puzzles |

**Recommendation:** Adopt **Alternative A: Elena Mercer**. Her quiet defiance in the Central Hall of Records grounds the game's emotional core in ordinary human dignity rather than high-tech heroics. (Mara Lin's darkroom techniques will be retained as a secondary witness/character in District 01).

### 2.3 Decision 3: The Antagonistic Force

#### Current Proposal: Department of Civil Reconciliation (DCR)
- **Why it exists:** Provides an active institutional entity enforcing the disappearance of non-conforming elements.
- **Weaknesses:** Sounds slightly generic / Orwellian (echoes *1984*'s Ministry of Love or *Severance*). Risks slipping into cartoonish totalitarian stormtroopers in trench coats.

#### Alternative A: The Bureau of Municipal Consistency (The Consistency Office) (RECOMMENDED)
- **Nature:** An unglamorous, humdrum municipal agency that sits somewhere between the Department of Public Works, the Tax Assessor, and Building Sanitation.
- **Visual & Behavioral Profile:** They do not carry machine guns or wear tactical masks. They wear ill-fitting grey tweed or wool coats, carry wooden measuring rules, carbon-copy clipboards, brass plumb bobs, and masonry chisels. They arrive in beige municipal panel vans.
- **Operational Logic:** They treat reality discrepancies as **structural nuisances or civic safety hazards**—like a gas leak, dry rot, or black mold. When a building or person is de-registered, their approach is apologetic, polite, and horrifyingly mundane: *"We apologize for the inconvenience, citizen, but your survey coordinates contain a cadastral error. Please vacate the premises so our crews can normalize the parcel."*
- **Thematic Weight:** The banality of evil. The horror is that everyone in the office is just doing their job to keep the city orderly.

#### Alternative B: The Consensus Compact (No Formal Agency — Social Complicity)
- **Nature:** There is no enforcement agency at all. The erasure is enforced entirely by the voluntary complicity of ordinary citizens.
- **Operational Logic:** The trauma of the past was so horrific that society signed a collective psychological treaty: whenever someone points out an anomaly, their neighbors, coworkers, and friends actively shush them, turn their backs, and ostracize them. 
- **Thematic Weight:** Highly psychological and disturbing.
- **Weaknesses:** Lacks a tangible, physical investigative adversary. It makes environmental puzzles harder to ground because there are no physical inspectors, sealed vans, or confiscated records for the player to intercept.

#### Recommendation:
Adopt **Alternative A: The Bureau of Municipal Consistency**. It provides tangible physical investigation targets (municipal notices, sealed crates, survey markers, inspection vans) while embodying the chilling banality of administrative routine.

### 2.4 Decision 4: The Historical Origin Event

#### Current Proposal: Project Recalibration
- **Weaknesses:** Sounds like a cold laboratory experiment. Lacks historical, political, and cultural texture.

#### Alternative A: The Great Settlement of 1968 (The Grand Compromise) (RECOMMENDED)
- **Historical Reality:** Fifty-eight years ago, Vesper and its surrounding valleys collapsed into a three-year civil war of horrific mutual atrocities, starvation, and sectarian slaughter (The Winter of Ashes). When peace was finally brokered, the rival factions realized that if every murdered family, stolen property, and burned district were brought to justice, trials and vengeance would continue for centuries, destroying any chance of survival.
- **The Pact:** The civic, ecclesiastical, and industrial leaders agreed to **The Grand Compromise**: the past would not simply be forgiven; it would be **systematically struck from public memory**. All records of the disputed districts, the liquidated populations, and the atrocities were physically interred in the deepest limestone vaults beneath the city. The city re-chartered itself with a clean ledger.
- **The Consequence:** What began as a legal and administrative consensus had an unanticipated, profound physical effect on Vesper's environment. When an entire society actively refuses to acknowledge a street, the street physically rots, empties, and slips into the sub-basement levels.

#### Alternative B: The Industrial Aquifer Collapse & Heavy Mineral Resonance
- **Historical Reality:** An environmental disaster caused by deep mining of a unique mineral ("Vesperite") that altered the groundwater table, causing localized spatial sinking and neurological memory fog.
- **Weaknesses:** Leans too heavily on environmental/toxicological sci-fi, diluting the moral and political weight of human choice and societal guilt.

#### Recommendation:
Adopt **Alternative A: The Great Settlement of 1968**. It gives the universe immense moral depth: the erasure was not invented by a comic-book villain, but by desperate human beings trying to stop endless bloodshed through radical forgetting.

### 2.5 Decision 5: The Reality Correction Mechanism

#### Current Proposal: Acoustic-Vibrational Dampening & Bureaucratic Consensus
- **Weaknesses:** If purely acoustic, why does ink disappear? If purely bureaucratic, how does stone physically shift? The mechanism must connect physical matter, human perception, and the administrative ledger without hand-waving.

#### Strongest Synthesized Solution: Inscriptional Materialism & The Subterranean Hum (RECOMMENDED)
- **The Mechanism:** Reality in Vesper operates on a **tripartite socio-material feedback loop**:
  1. *The Geological Substrate:* Vesper is constructed from a unique, highly dense ironstone/limestone aggregate quarried from The Deep ("St. Jude’s Limestone"). This stone possesses high piezoelectric memory—it physically stabilizes under continuous, organized acoustic vibration.
  2. *The Subterranean Infrasound (The Hum):* Beneath Vesper, the massive municipal ventilation turbines, subterranean drainage pumps, and rotary converters installed during the 1968 Settlement continuously hum at **18.4 Hz** (the human ocular and vestibular resonance frequency). Over decades, this constant infrasound acts as a cognitive dampener: it suppresses short-term hippocampal memory consolidation regarding visual incongruities. Citizens instinctively look away from things that don't fit; their brains smooth over the seam.
  3. *The Ledger Principle (Material Follows Inscription):* Human habitation and maintenance are what keep physical structures stable. When the Bureau of Consistency de-registers a house, water, electricity, and coal delivery are cut. But more critically, the Infrasound creates an aversion field: citizens stop looking at it, walking past it, or touching it. Deprived of human sensory reinforcement and subject to the 18.4 Hz vibration, the piezoelectric limestone loses its structural coherence, slowly sinking into the subterranean voids of The Deep.
- **Why this works:** It unifies geology, acoustics, human psychology, and municipal administration into a single, cohesive, physical reality that has hard, testable limits.

### 2.6 Decision 6: The Preservation Mechanism

#### Current Proposal: The Residual Virus
- **Weaknesses:** "Virus" sounds digital or biological. In a 1970s/80s analog municipal setting, it feels anachronistic and like a trope from cyberpunk rather than a tactile cinematic mystery.

#### Strongest Solution: Physical Counter-Anchors & The Black Registry (RECOMMENDED)
- **The Mechanism:**
  1. *Material Anchors:* Materials that are non-reactive, non-piezoelectric, and immune to 18.4 Hz resonance: **cold-cast bronze, virgin lead, vulcanized rubber, and silver-halide emulsion**.
  2. *The Black Registry:* Elena Mercer bound duplicate municipal carbon-slips inside heavy lead-sheathed binding covers. Because lead blocks the vibrational entrainment and protects the paper from damp air, the ink and carbon cannot fade.
  3. *Foundational Tuning Pins:* Clara Reed / Elena hammered cold-cast bronze tuning pins into the cornerstone foundations of key buildings across District 01 and Greyhaven. These pins act as vibrational cancels—deadening the 18.4 Hz hum within a 15-meter radius. Inside these pockets, **the past cannot be erased**, and anyone stepping into them feels an immediate, dizzying return of clarity and memory.

### 2.7 Decision 7: The Three Universe Laws (Refined for Gameplay)

The three abstract laws are reformulated into **concrete, gameplay-facing mechanical principles**:

1. **The Law of Matter Over Ink (The Conservation of Physical Trace):**  
   *What is written on paper can be erased; what is cast in bronze or pressed into stone endures.*  
   *Gameplay Meaning:* Written documents may contradict each other, but physical tools, tool marks, wear patterns, key mechanisms, and structural foundations never lie. The player uses physical observation to defeat bureaucratic deception.
2. **The Law of the Displaced Void (The Null Constant):**  
   *Nothing is destroyed; it is only relocated to where the light cannot reach.*  
   *Gameplay Meaning:* When a room or railway platform is removed from the street level, its physical mass still exists. It is bricked up, paved over, or dropped into the sub-basement infrastructure of The Deep. Every missing space can be physically found if the player tracks the plumbing, wiring, and structural load lines.
3. **The Law of the Contradiction Spike (The Mnemonic Shockwave):**  
   *When a living witness who remembers encounters an undeniable physical anchor in the presence of an erased consensus, reality fractures locally.*  
   *Gameplay Meaning:* When Thomas brings a preserved physical artifact into a room where someone has forgotten it, the witness suffers nausea, confusion, or a sudden flood of tears. If forced too hard, the local environment flickers (lights blow out, plaster cracks, shadows detach), attracting the attention of Consistency inspectors.

## 3. COMPREHENSIVE UNIVERSE MECHANICS: THE TEN CORE QUESTIONS

To ensure the universe never relies on vague supernatural excuses, every fundamental rule is answered definitively:

### Q1: What is being changed?
**Answer:** Three things simultaneously:
1. *The Administrative Record:* Birth entries, cadastral surveys, property deeds, tax rolls, and maps.
2. *The Collective Memory:* The short-term and autobiographical recall of ordinary citizens, constantly smoothed over by the municipal infrasound hum.
3. *The Physical Architecture:* Physical buildings and spaces whose maintenance is halted, leading to accelerated structural subsidence into subterranean chambers.

### Q2: What causes the changes?
**Answer:** A deliberate, coordinated municipal procedure initiated by the **Bureau of Municipal Consistency**. It begins with an administrative decree (a "De-Registration Warrant"), followed by physical isolation, record redaction, and the application of targeted acoustic dampening to suppress public attention while demolition or walling-off occurs.

### Q3: Why does the world need correction?
**Answer:** To prevent the return of **The Winter of Ashes** (the catastrophic 1968 civil slaughter). Vesper's modern economy and peace are built entirely upon the fiction that certain atrocities never happened, certain families never held title to the land, and certain districts never existed. Acknowledging the truth would immediately reignite legal claims, blood feuds, and civic collapse.

### Q4: Who or what controls the process?
**Answer:** A tripartite civic council:
- *The Municipal Records Directorate* (administers the paperwork).
- *The Public Works Consistency Division* (executes the physical walling-off and demolition).
- *The Sub-Basement Engineering Guild* (maintains the infrasonic ventilation and drainage turbines in The Deep).

### Q5: What are the physical limitations of the process?
**Answer:**
1. *Distance:* Beyond the range of Vesper’s ventilation turbines (e.g., in rural Greyhaven), the infrasound is weak. People remember much more, and physical buildings do not sink as rapidly.
2. *Material Density:* Lead, bronze, dense iron, and silver-halide glass plates do not respond to the vibrational resonance.
3. *Subterranean Capacity:* The Deep is filling up. Displaced architectural debris, walled-off trains, and un-reconciled material have accumulated over 50 years. The structural pressure is reaching a geological breaking point.

### Q6: Why do Residuals survive?
**Answer:** Residuals survive when they are:
- Made of non-piezoelectric materials (lead, cast iron, bronze).
- Physically shielded inside dead zones (behind thick brickwork, underground vaults, or near acoustic tuning pins).
- Tightly held by human emotional and physical habits (e.g., a signalman's hands automatically reaching for an unlisted lever out of 20 years of muscle memory).

### Q7: What determines which memories persist?
**Answer:** **Kinesthetic and Muscle Memory over Semantic Memory.**  
The infrasound primarily attacks cognitive, semantic recall (names, dates, faces in the abstract). It is far less effective against **physical somatic habit**: a pianist can still play a piece taught by an erased teacher; a signalman's hand reaches for a switch at 03:14 AM; a mother still reaches to set a fourth plate at dinner before catching herself in confusion.

### Q8: What happens when contradictory evidence accumulates?
**Answer:** **A Cadastral Tremor (Ontological Fracture).**  
When too many physical contradictions exist in a single location, the municipal consensus fails. Plaster falls from walls, revealing old brickwork; sound echoes with a 5-second delay; shadows fall in directions opposite to light fixtures; and the boundary into **The Outside** opens, causing spatial geometry to loop or distort.

### Q9: Can the phenomenon be interrupted?
**Answer:** **Yes.**  
1. Locally: By neutralizing the infrasound (destroying or dampening local resonance vents, planting bronze tuning pins).
2. Globally: By breaching the Central Turbine Chambers in The Deep and publicly publishing the intact carbon-copy records of *The Black Registry*.

### Q10: What are the consequences of intervention?
**Answer:** **The Return of the Fracture.**  
If the system is interrupted without care:
- Millions of erased memories will flood back simultaneously, causing mass psychological disorientation, grief, and civil unrest.
- Walled-off, displaced architectural masses in The Deep will structurally shift, causing sinkholes and structural collapses across modern Vesper City.
- Society will be forced to confront its true history and rebuild without the comfortable anesthesia of state-enforced forgetting.

### 4.1 Act-by-Act Dramatic & Informational Beat Sheet

#### ACT I: THE RETURN (Focus: Intimate Disorientation & Paranoia)
- **Cinematic Opening:** Thomas Mercer steps off the night train at Vesper Central. Rain, grey fog, heavy iron girders, halogen streetlights.
- **The First Clue:** Thomas arrives at his apartment on Krupp Street. His brass key binds in the lock. The door opens: a strange man wearing pajamas stares at him in confusion. The man’s name is on the electric bill. Thomas’s name is nowhere.
- **The Investigation:** Thomas retreats to his railway signal tower at West Junction. In his personal locker, his daughter Elena's winter coat is still hanging. Inside the lining, he finds a railway parcel receipt and a tarnished bronze key stamped **"ARCHIVE 09 - LOWER LEVEL."**
- **The Climax of Act I:** At 03:14 AM, the track relay clicks. An unregistered train rolls past in the fog—silent, blacked out, its windows boarded up with galvanized sheet metal.

#### ACT II: THE INCONSISTENCY (Focus: Physical Contradiction & Greyhaven)
- **The Discovery:** Thomas traces the parcel receipt to an abandoned postal depot on the boundary of District 01. The building is officially marked for "Cadastral Rectification" by the Consistency Office.
- **Investigation Gameplay:** Thomas navigates the building before demolition crews arrive. Behind a fake plasterboard wall, he finds Elena’s first hidden stash: carbon slips documenting hundreds of citizens whose names were erased over the last six months.
- **The Greyhaven Journey:** Elena’s diary entries mention an old family house in rural Greyhaven. Thomas travels there by local bus. In Greyhaven, outside the reach of the city’s infrasound, the local villagers remember Elena vividly, but they speak of her in the past tense, believing she died ten years ago in an industrial accident that Thomas knows never happened.
- **The Climax of Act II:** Thomas enters the Greyhaven house. The house is physically split: one half is rotting timber and ivy, while the other half contains freshly brewed coffee, lit candles, and Elena's handwritten letters dated three days ago.

#### ACT III: THE RESIDUAL (Focus: The Under-City & The Apparatus)
- **Descent into The Deep:** Guided by Elena's notes, Thomas discovers a forgotten maintenance elevator beneath the Greyhaven railway embankment that plunges hundreds of meters down into the subterranean infrastructure beneath Vesper.
- **The Environment:** The Deep is vast, cavernous, and terrifyingly industrial. Thomas walks through entire streets of District 09 that were physically swallowed forty years ago—intact storefronts, vintage streetcars, and municipal libraries standing in absolute darkness beneath vaulted limestone ceilings.
- **The Encounter:** Thomas encounters **Inspector Janos Vance**, a veteran Consistency auditor who is not an evil villain, but an exhausted, aging man carrying a clipboard and a thermos. Janos explains the reality of the 1968 Settlement: *"If we let that girl bring those names back to the surface, Thomas, the families will want their blood back. The city will burn within forty-eight hours."*

#### ACT IV: THE REVELATION (Focus: The Absence & Personal Truth)
- **The Truth of Elena:** Thomas reaches the Core Archive in The Deep. He discovers Elena did not escape into hiding. To protect the records from being burned, she sealed herself inside the hermetic Lead Vault alongside *The Black Registry*.
- **The Personal Gut-Punch:** Thomas finds audio recordings left by Elena. He learns the hardest truth of all: **Thomas himself had once worked as a senior signal inspector who cleared the tracks for the initial 1968 liquidations.** In grief and guilt, Thomas had voluntarily taken the infrasonic treatment decades ago to forget his own complicity. Elena had discovered her father's erased past, and her rebellion was an act of both justice and redemption for him.

#### ACT V: THE CHOICE (Focus: Philosophical & Systemic Climax)
- **The Three Endings:**
  1. **PRESERVATION (The Silent Agreement):** Thomas reseals the Lead Vault, steps into the Consistency Office, hands over his bronze key, and accepts the infrasonic treatment once more. He awakens back in his signal tower at dawn. The 03:14 AM train never existed. He lives in peaceful, tragic oblivion.
  2. **RECOGNITION (The Broken Consensus):** Thomas activates the auxiliary rail power, uncoupling the lead-vault train cars and driving them up through the sealed tunnels, crashing through the floor of Vesper Central Station in broad daylight. Thousands of carbon records scatter into the crowded concourse. The amnesia is broken; the city is thrown into chaotic awakening, grief, and truth.
  3. **RELEASE (The Unwritten Boundary):** Thomas refuses both the cruel amnesia of the city and the violent chaos of total exposure. He sabotages the subterranean turbines, severing the infrasound loop, and walks with Elena’s records out through the spatial collapse of **The Outside**, seeking whatever exists beyond the unwritten horizon.

## 5. REVALIDATION OF THE TECHNICAL FOUNDATION

### 5.3 Technical Guardrails & Performance Budgets
Operating on a GeForce G210 with 1GB VRAM requires rigorous engineering discipline:
1. **Renderer:** Exclusively `gl_compatibility` (OpenGL 3.3).
2. **Texture Atlasing:** Maximum diffuse/normal/roughness texture resolution: **1024x1024** for major architectural surfaces; **512x512** for props. Use combined packed textures (ORM: Occlusion/Roughness/Metallic in RGB channels) to minimize texture samplers.
3. **Draw Call Budget:** Strict limit of **≤ 180 - 250 draw calls per frame**. Aggressive use of Godot `MultiMeshInstance3D` for repeated modular elements (rail tracks, streetlights, bricks, cobblestones).
4. **Polygon Budget:**
   - Playable Protagonist: ≤ 8,000 triangles.
   - Secondary NPCs: ≤ 4,000 triangles.
   - Hero Props (Clocks, signal levers, desks): ≤ 1,500 triangles.
   - Modular Wall/Floor blocks: ≤ 200 triangles.
5. **Lighting & Shadows:**
   - No dynamic real-time shadows on point/omni lights.
   - Exactly ONE directional light (moonlight or street-level key) with low-resolution shadow maps (1024x1024).
   - Interior lighting baked via lightmaps or simulated using vertex color tinting and unshaded ambient probes.
6. **Blender-Free Pipeline:**
   - Modular geometry constructed via Godot CSG and exported to `.tscn`.
   - Complex props generated via Tripo AI / Meshy, downloaded as `.glb`, and processed through custom Python optimization scripts (`07_ASSETS/scripts/optimize_glb.py`) to reduce vertex density and clamp textures before engine import.

## 6. NARRATIVE RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Assessment | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone Fatigue (Relentless Misery)** | A story about amnesia, state gaslighting, and loss can feel oppressive and depressing over several hours. | **Inject Warmth and Intimacy:** Moments of quiet humanity: sharing hot chicory coffee with an old signalman; dry, wry humor between railway workers; poignant childhood memories preserved in Elena's diary; the tactile satisfaction of fixing mechanical clocks. |
| **Complexity Overload** | Multiple layers of past atrocities and historical treaties could confuse the player. | **Tactile Anchor Principle:** Every complex historical event must be represented by a single, physical, tactile object the player can turn over in their hands (a brass medal, an inked stamp, a pocket compass, a train ticket). |
| **Pacing Lulls in Act III** | Wandering through vast underground tunnels in The Deep can become repetitive and spatially monotonous. | **Diverse Sub-Biomes in The Deep:** The Deep is not just grey rock; it contains distinct historical strata: Victorian pneumatic-tube stations, submerged waterworks with reflections, warm vacuum-tube server chambers, and silent preserved neighborhoods from 1968. |
